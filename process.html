The process

Four steps.
One finished song.

No jargon. No confusing back-and-forth. Just your idea, and then a track you're proud to release.

01
Step one
Send your starting point

Whatever you have — send it. There's genuinely no wrong format. We've worked from all of these:

A voice note on your phone (even 10 seconds is fine)
A GarageBand or Logic rough demo
Lyrics in a Google Doc with a description
A reference track and a description of the vibe
Just a concept or a feeling in words

What happens next

We listen, ask a few targeted questions (genre, mood, references), then get to work. Usually within a few hours of receiving your material.

02
Step two · within 48 hours
First draft lands in your inbox

Within 48 hours you'll hear a fully produced version of your track. Not a rough sketch — a real, produced draft with:

Full arrangement (drums, bass, melodic elements)
Your vocal/melody sitting in the mix
A reference mix to judge tone and direction
Notes on our creative decisions so you understand the choices
03
Step three · ongoing
Refine together — unlimited rounds

You give feedback. We revise. We keep going until the song feels exactly right to you. There is no revision cap.

Change the arrangement, energy, instruments
Adjust the mix balance or tonal character
Rewrite sections if the direction shifts
Try multiple versions of a chorus or bridge

How most projects go

Most artists need 2–4 revision rounds. Some need 1. Some need 8. We've never had a project go over because of "too many changes."

04
Step four · final delivery
Release-ready files, delivered

Once you're happy, we deliver the full mastered package. Everything you need to publish your track anywhere, immediately.

WAV 24-bit master (for distribution)
MP3 320kbps (for sharing/streaming)
Stems if requested (separate instrument tracks)
Loudness-normalised for Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube
The craft behind the sound
Every fader. Every decision.
Made for your song.
