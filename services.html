FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to send to get started?

Anything — a voice note, a rough demo, lyrics in a doc, or even just a description of what you're going for. We work from wherever you are.

How do revisions work?

There's no revision cap. You give feedback, we revise. We keep going until the song feels exactly right to you — that's the whole point.

Do I own my song?

Yes, 100%. You retain full creative and commercial ownership. We don't take publishing cuts or royalty shares. It's your song.

How long does the full process take?

You'll have a first draft within 48 hours. The full process — including revisions and mastering — typically takes 7–14 days depending on the number of revision rounds.

What formats do I get at the end?

WAV (24-bit master), MP3 (320kbps), and any streaming-specific formats you need. Everything you need to distribute immediately.
