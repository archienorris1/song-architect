Maya's story:
from voice note to 12k streams

Maya had been sitting on a song for two years. She'd tried recording it herself, hired a producer who didn't get the vibe, and was about to give up entirely.

She sent us a 30-second voice note from her phone. Here's what happened next:

Day 1 — She sent the voice memo

30 seconds of her humming the melody with a rough chord progression on acoustic guitar.

Day 3 — First draft delivered

A full R&B production with her melody sitting in a track that sounded like a real release. She cried listening to it.

Day 9 — Final master delivered

Two revision rounds later — a brighter chorus, more low-end in the second verse — the mastered file was in her hands.

Week 3 — Live on Spotify

The song she'd tried to make for two years hit Spotify. It's now at 12,000 streams and counting.
